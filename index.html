Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 3/23/2026
App Name: HIIT Stations

This Privacy Policy describes how HIIT Stations (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to minimize data collection. Our app is designed to function without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

Information you provide:

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information.

Automatically collected information:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data (screens viewed, features used)
  • Performance data (crash logs, error reports)
  • Approximate location (derived from region settings, not GPS)

2. How We Use Information

  • Improve app performance and reliability.
  • Fix bugs and prevent crashes.
  • Understand how users interact with workouts.
  • Improve future updates and functionality.

3. Workout Data

The app allows you to track workouts and personal performance metrics. This data:

  • Is stored locally on your device.
  • Is not shared with us unless you explicitly choose to export it.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited, anonymous information with third-party services (like Google Analytics or Sentry) to help operate the app.

5. Data Storage and Security

Most data remains on your device. We do not maintain a central user database.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices

  • Use the app without providing any personal data.
  • Disable analytics in your device settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all local workout data.
