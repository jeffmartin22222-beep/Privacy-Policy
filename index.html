Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 3/23/2026
App Name: HIIT Stations

This Privacy Policy describes how HIIT Stations (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We aim to minimize data collection. Our app is designed to function without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

Information you provide:
We do not require you to create an account.
We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information.

Automatically collected information:
Device information, app usage data, performance data, and approximate location (non-precise).

2. How We Use Information

  • Improve app performance
  • Fix bugs
  • Understand usage
  • Improve features

3. Workout Data

Workout data is stored locally on your device and is not shared unless you choose to export or share it.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Some anonymous data may be shared with analytics or crash reporting services.

5. Data Storage and Security

Most data remains on your device. We do not maintain a central database.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics and performance monitoring.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13.

8. Your Choices

  • Use the app without providing personal data

  • Delete the app to remove local data

9. Changes to This Policy
