HIIT Stations

Build custom HIIT workouts, timed stations, and interval routines with a simple, guided workout planner designed to keep you moving.

About the App

HIIT Stations helps you create and follow customizable workout plans for interval training, circuit sessions, and timed exercise stations. Whether you're training at home, in the gym, or coaching a group, HIIT Stations makes it easy to stay organized and on pace.

Features

  • Custom HIIT workout builder
  • Timed exercise stations
  • Interval and circuit workout support
  • Exercise guidance during sessions
  • Save and reuse workout plans
